Whats Cyberpunk 2077?

Cyberpunk 2077 is an open-world RPG shooter set in the year 2077 in Night City—a place where technology has advanced to the point that people can install mechanical enhancements known as "Cyberware"—the story follows V, a mercenary determined to make a name for themselves. V teams up with others to pull off the heist of a lifetime, hoping to break into the big leagues; however, things go wrong, and V ends up implicated in the murder of one of the world’s most prominent figures. Exposed, V is forced to flee with the object they were hired to steal: the "Relic." In the ensuing chaos, V’s employer betrays them, attempting to kill them. The rest of the game centers on V's struggle to survive and get rid of the Relic, which is causing them to gradually transform into someone else.

Why Cyberpunk 2077 is in third place?

Cyberpunk is in third place because this game is not purely about shooting, but also holds this spot for having been a very ambitious project that, although it launched very poorly due to the amount of bugs, was a project made with care thanks to the incredible characters and stories the game presents to us. Furthermore, the versatility of choices and playstyles in this game is quite varied and a lot of fun, since you can be anything from a stealthy assassin to a netrunner who defeats enemies by hacking them; on top of all this, its art style and world are exceptionally well-built.
