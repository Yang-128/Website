HOLLOW KNIGHT

Explore the forgotten kingdom of Hallownest

DeveloperTeam Cherry
ReleasedFebruary 2017
GenreMetroidvania
PlatformsPC, Switch, PS, Xbox

What is Hollow Knight?

Hollow Knight is a 2D action-adventure game made by Team Cherry, a tiny indie studio from Adelaide, Australia, with only three core members. You play as the Knight, a small, silent bug warrior who comes to the ruined underground kingdom of Hallownest. The kingdom used to be great, but now it is falling apart because of a mysterious infection that has driven its people mad.

The game is a "Metroidvania": one huge, connected world that you explore at your own pace. As you find new abilities, like dashing, wall-climbing and double-jumping, you can reach places that were blocked before. Along the way you fight hard enemies and more than 40 bosses, meet strange characters, and slowly piece together the sad story of what happened to Hallownest.

The art is all hand-drawn, and the music by Christopher Larkin is calm, sad and epic at the same time. Even with all that, the game only cost about $15 at launch, and every expansion was released for free.

Why did it go viral?

  • Small team, huge game. It started as a small Kickstarter project, but people were shocked that three developers could make a world this big and polished. Players often spend 40+ hours just to finish it, and much longer to get 100%.
  • Word of mouth. It didn't have a big marketing budget. It spread because players kept recommending it to friends, on Reddit and on YouTube, and it became known as one of the best indie games ever made.
  • Hard but fair. The difficulty makes beating a boss feel amazing. Clips of tough fights, like the Radiance or the Pantheons in the Godmaster update, became a big part of YouTube and Twitch.
  • Mystery and lore. The story is hidden in item descriptions, dialogue and environments, so fans made tons of theory videos trying to explain it.
  • Speedrunning. Hollow Knight became one of the most popular speedrun games, with runners finding crazy glitches and shortcuts.
  • The Silksong meme. The sequel, Hollow Knight: Silksong, was announced in 2019, and then fans waited years with almost no news. "Any Silksong news?" turned into one of the biggest memes in gaming. When Silksong finally launched in September 2025, so many people rushed to buy it that online stores struggled to keep up, and the original game got a huge new wave of players.

Why is it #5 on our list?

05 Hollow Knight

Hollow Knight earns its spot in our Top 5 because it proves that a game doesn't need a giant studio or a huge budget to be a masterpiece. Its world, art, music and combat are as good as games made by teams of hundreds of people.

So why not higher? The games above it are massive, story-driven experiences with full voice acting, huge open worlds and tons of player choice. Hollow Knight is also very difficult, and its map system can be confusing, which can turn away some new players. It isn't for everyone, but for the people who love it, it's one of the best games they've ever played.

"No cost too great." — Hollow Knight is proof that a small team with a big vision can create something unforgettable.
