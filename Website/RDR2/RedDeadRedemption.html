Red Dead Redemption 2 #2

Red Dead Redemption 2 is an open world game set in 1899, a time when the era of cowboys and outlaws was coming to an end, giving way to the industrial age. In this story, you follow Arthur Morgan, a cowboy who has spent his entire life in a gang of outlaws. He has become selfish, often taking advantage of those outside his gang driven by a philosophy that prioritizes the gang above all else, viewing it much like a family. However, due to certain events that unfold throughout the story (I wont include any spoilers, as the game features an excellent narrative that is best experienced firsthand), his mindset shifts, and he becomes less selfish.

Why Red dead redemption is in second place?

*Red Dead Redemption* takes second place, thanks to its gameplay and the story it delivers. Since you dont create your own character but rather step into one already crafted by the game the narrative carries greater impact, allowing the player to fully appreciate the protagonists redemption arc as he evolves from a selfish individual into a kind hearted person. *Red Dead Redemption* shines through its story, realism, and the world in which it is set; almost every action you take carries a consequence. The game exhibits an extraordinary level of attention to detail especially remarkable for the time of its release (2018). The gameplay is also highly engaging; while it isnt a frenetic, run and gun experience, the combat is deliberate and precise. For instance, instead of wielding a submachine gun that fires dozens of rounds, you rely on a revolver the action may not be fast paced, but every single shot feels impactful.
