Baldur's Gate 3

Discover the world behind our #1 ranked game

What makes Baldur's Gate 3 so special? From its incredible storytelling to its strategic combat system, there is much more to discover.

1. Story and Setting

Baldur's Gate 3 takes place in the Forgotten Realms, a fantasy universe inspired by Dungeons & Dragons. Players begin their journey after being captured by mysterious Mind Flayers, who implant a parasite inside their brains. As the story unfolds, players must find a way to remove the parasite while becoming involved in a much larger conflict threatening the world. The journey is filled with unexpected encounters, dangerous enemies, and difficult decisions.

2. Character Creation

One of the most impressive aspects of the game is its character customization system. Players can create their own protagonist by choosing different races, classes, appearances, and abilities. Each combination can significantly influence the way the game is played.


Some available classes include:

  • Barbarian – A powerful warrior who relies on strength.
  • Wizard – A spellcaster with a wide range of magical abilities.
  • Rogue – A specialist in stealth and precision.
  • Paladin – A holy warrior combining combat and magic.
  • Ranger – A skilled fighter who specializes in exploration.

3. Combat System

Unlike many traditional action games, Baldur's Gate 3 uses turn-based combat. Players and enemies take turns making decisions during battles. The combat system is based on the fifth edition of Dungeons & Dragons. Players must carefully consider their positioning, abilities, spells, and surroundings before making a move. Creativity is often rewarded. For example, players can use environmental objects and different elemental effects to gain an advantage over their enemies.

4. Choices and Consequences

Perhaps the game's greatest strength is the freedom it gives players. Almost every important decision can influence the storyline and relationships between characters. Players can negotiate with enemies, solve problems through dialogue, help strangers, or make decisions that completely change the direction of their journey. There is no single correct way to complete the game. Different decisions can lead to dramatically different experiences and endings.

5. Exploration and World Design

The world of Baldur's Gate 3 is filled with hidden locations, mysterious caves, ancient ruins, and interesting characters. Exploration is not simply about travelling from one mission to another. Players can discover secret paths, interact with objects, uncover hidden quests, and encounter unexpected events. The game rewards curiosity and experimentation.

6. Multiplayer Experience

Baldur's Gate 3 can also be played cooperatively with up to four players. Friends can create their own characters, explore the world together, fight enemies, and make important decisions as a team. However, different players may have different opinions about how to approach a situation. This makes multiplayer adventures unpredictable and often entertaining.

Why Is It Our #1 Choice?

Baldur's Gate 3 combines storytelling, freedom, strategy, exploration, and character development into one remarkable experience. Its greatest achievement is allowing players to create their own stories rather than simply following a predetermined path. Every playthrough can feel different, making it a game that players can return to again and again.
