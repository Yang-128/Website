Whats Cyberpunk 2077?

Cyberpunk 2077 is a openworld rpg shooter Set in the year 2077 in Night City—a place where technology has advanced to the point that people can install mechanical enhancements known as "Cyberware"—the story follows V, a mercenary determined to make a name for themselves. V teams up with others to pull off the heist of a lifetime, hoping to break into the big leagues; however, things go wrong, and V ends up implicated in the murder of one of the world’s most prominent figures. Exposed, V is forced to flee with the object they were hired to steal: the "Relic." In the ensuing chaos, V’s employer betrays them, attempting to kill them. The rest of the game centers on V’s struggle to survive and get rid of the Relic, which is causing them to gradually transform into someone else.
